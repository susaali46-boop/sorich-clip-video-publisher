The Sorich standard

Clarity over noise.
Control over guesswork.

We believe creator software should be honest about what is connected, what is processing and what is actually published. Sorich will never label a post “live” unless the platform confirms it. Your accounts and content remain under your control.

Good to know

Before you
get started.

Can I connect Google Drive and TikTok right now?

The current workspace includes sign-in and private workspace records. Secure Drive OAuth, video transfer and TikTok publishing still need server-side integration and platform approval before those controls can be enabled.

Can Sorich publish publicly to TikTok?

Only when TikTok has approved the app and the connected account is eligible. Until then, Sorich must use the upload methods TikTok permits and show their real status.

Is my workspace public?

No. Each signed-in creator gets a private workspace. Workspace data is intended to be scoped through Supabase row-level security.
