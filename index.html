Platform permissions matter.
TikTok controls which posting methods are available. Draft uploads and direct public posting have different requirements; direct posting may require TikTok review and audit approval. Sorich AI will not bypass those requirements.
Creator control

Clear permissions.
Thoughtful automation.

Sorich AI is designed to request only the access needed for the features you choose. You decide which account to connect and which content to submit. You can revoke connected-account access through the relevant provider's settings.
