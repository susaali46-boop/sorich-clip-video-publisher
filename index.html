This site describes a personal workflow used to prepare and publish short-form videos to the Sorich-clip YouTube channel (@sorich-clip).

What the application does

The workflow is intended for the channel owner's use. It helps prepare video uploads and publishing details such as titles, descriptions, tags, and visibility settings using connected services.

It is not offered as a public upload service and is not intended to manage YouTube channels for other people.

Privacy and data handling

The workflow may process video files and related metadata selected for publishing, using connected Google/YouTube and automation services. This website itself does not provide an upload form or ask visitors to submit personal information.
