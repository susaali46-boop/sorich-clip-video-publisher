What this project does

The workflow is intended to publish owner-selected video files and associated metadata, such as titles, descriptions, tags, and visibility settings, to the owner's channel using authorized services. It is not a public upload service, does not offer public registration, and is not intended to manage other people's YouTube channels.

Depending on the workflow configuration, connected services may include Google/YouTube and automation or file-storage providers authorized by the owner. Only services needed for the selected task should be connected.
