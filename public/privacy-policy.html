Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sorich Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit the website or use the creator workspace.

Current feature status

The current workspace supports account access, private video-file storage, draft preparation, and provider-account connection in configured test environments. Private-library uploads are stored in your workspace; they are not YouTube uploads. The automated long-video clipping feature and YouTube video-publishing feature are currently disabled/not available in the production application. A YouTube connection may be tested only in a configured preview environment.

1. Contact

Sorich Studio is an independent creator-software project. For privacy questions or requests, contact alisusa169@gmail.com. Never send passwords, API keys, OAuth access tokens, or other secrets by email.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication/session information.
  • Workspace information: private media-library records, original filenames, file types, file sizes, draft titles, captions, hashtags, planned dates, and workspace settings.
  • Connected-account information: if you connect a supported provider in a configured environment, we process the account or channel identifier, display name, granted permission scopes, token expiry information, and encrypted authorization credentials needed to maintain or disconnect the connection.
  • Technical information: security logs, diagnostic information, browser/device details, and request metadata needed to operate and protect the service.

3. Google and YouTube API data

If you connect a Google/YouTube account in an environment where the integration is configured, Sorich Studio requests the permissions shown on Google's consent screen. The current YouTube connection flow requests basic Google identity and YouTube read/upload permissions so the application can identify the connected channel and support a future authorized publishing workflow. The production application does not currently provide a YouTube video-upload feature.

We use Google/YouTube data only for the user-facing account-connection and channel-identification functions described above, and for a future publishing feature only after it is implemented and enabled. We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for generalized AI/ML model training. Human access is limited to support requested by you, security investigations, legal compliance, or other circumstances permitted by Google's policies.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

4. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide private workspace storage and draft tools; connect and manage provider accounts when configured; provide support; prevent abuse; maintain service reliability; and comply with legal obligations. We do not upload private-library videos to YouTube from the current production application.

5. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information as needed to operate the service, including Supabase (authentication, database, and private storage) and Vercel (hosting and application delivery). If you connect YouTube in a configured test environment, Google receives the authorization request and information necessary to complete the connection. We do not authorize providers to use Google API data for advertising or generalized AI model training.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Workspace files are stored in private storage associated with your workspace. OAuth credentials, when collected, are encrypted before storage and are not returned to the browser. We use access controls and provider security features intended to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information while needed to provide the service, maintain your account, meet security requirements, or comply with law. You may request deletion as described below.

7. Disconnecting Google and revoking access

If you connected Google/YouTube, use the account-disconnect control when available in the workspace, or revoke Sorich Studio from Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents future access through that authorization; it does not automatically delete information already stored by Sorich Studio.

8. Data deletion and privacy requests

To request deletion of your Sorich Studio account or workspace information, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page or email alisusa169@gmail.com. We may verify account ownership before acting. Deleting Sorich Studio information does not delete content independently stored by Google or YouTube.

9. Children's privacy

Sorich Studio is not directed to children under 13. Users must meet the minimum age required by applicable law and the relevant provider terms.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy as the service changes. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.
