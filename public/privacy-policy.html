SORICH STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how Sorich Studio handles information when you use its private creator workspace, account connections and YouTube publishing tools.

Current feature status

The workspace supports private media storage, drafts, configured provider-account connections and a YouTube upload workflow. The separate automated long-video clipping feature remains disabled. YouTube uploads are made only after the user selects a source file, reviews its metadata and settings, confirms rights and terms, and presses the upload button.

1. Contact

Privacy requests: alisusa169@gmail.com. Never email passwords, API keys or OAuth tokens.

2. Information we process

  • Account identifiers, email, display name and authentication/session information.
  • Private workspace files, filenames, MIME types, file sizes, storage paths and media records.
  • Draft and upload metadata such as titles, descriptions, tags, visibility, made-for-kids declaration, upload status and YouTube video IDs.
  • Connected-account identifiers, channel names, granted scopes, token expiry data and encrypted OAuth credentials.
  • Security logs, diagnostic information and request metadata.

3. Google and YouTube API data

When you connect YouTube, Sorich Studio requests Google identity and YouTube channel/upload permissions shown on Google's consent screen. The application uses channel information to identify your connected channel, and uses the YouTube upload permission only when you explicitly approve an upload. It retrieves status only for videos uploaded through this workspace.

Google/YouTube data is not sold, used for targeted advertising, or used for generalized AI/ML model training. OAuth credentials are encrypted server-side and are not sent to the browser. Access is limited to service operation, user-requested support, security, and legal compliance.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use, and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How data is used

We use information to authenticate users, operate private storage and drafts, connect YouTube accounts, upload user-approved videos, retrieve upload status, provide support, protect the service and comply with law. We do not upload private-library files without a separate explicit upload action.

5. Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database and private storage. Vercel hosts and delivers the application. Google processes OAuth requests and YouTube API requests when a user connects or uploads. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

6. Storage, security and retention

Workspace files and records are stored in private workspace storage. OAuth credentials are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before storage and are accessible only to server-side code. We retain information while needed to provide the service, maintain security, or meet legal obligations. We apply access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Revocation and deletion

You can disconnect a channel in the workspace or revoke Sorich Studio at Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents future access; it does not automatically remove information already stored by Sorich Studio. To request deletion of your account or workspace data, follow the Data Deletion instructions or email alisusa169@gmail.com. We will process deletions as soon as reasonably possible and in accordance with applicable YouTube API requirements, including the applicable 30-day deadline for API Data deletion after revocation.

8. Children's privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13. Users must meet the minimum age requirements applicable to their account, location and provider terms. A parent or eligible account owner must manage any required developer-account or quota-review responsibilities.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as features change. The revised date appears at the top.
