Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sorich Studio ("Sorich Studio," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit our website or use our creator workspace. It also explains how Google and YouTube data will be handled if and when the relevant YouTube API integration is enabled.

Current integration status

Accounts and workspace features are under development. The current application does not yet offer a working Google/YouTube OAuth connection or YouTube video-upload feature. We do not currently receive YouTube API data through this application. YouTube-related processing described below applies only after the relevant feature is implemented, approved, and you choose to connect your account.

1. Who operates the service and how to contact us

Sorich Studio is an independent creator-software project. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact alisusa169@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, OAuth tokens, API keys, or other secrets in your request.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account information: email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication/session information needed to create and secure your workspace.
  • Workspace information: content-library entries, project settings, draft titles, descriptions, captions, hashtags, clip preferences, job status, timestamps, and error information.
  • Video information: a source video URL or file that you submit, selected timestamps and editing settings, and generated clip URLs or metadata. The current clip-generation endpoint may send a submitted YouTube video URL and selected processing settings to the configured video-analysis/processing provider (currently Reka AI) to perform the requested task.
  • Technical information: security and diagnostic logs, browser/device details, and basic request metadata needed to protect and operate the service.

3. YouTube and Google API Services

If a YouTube integration is enabled in a future release and you authorize it, Sorich Studio will request only the Google/YouTube permissions shown on Google's consent screen and needed for the feature you choose. Depending on the approved scopes and feature, this may include your YouTube channel identifier and basic channel information, video metadata, and information needed to upload a video or check the status of an upload that you explicitly request.

We will use YouTube API data only to provide and improve the user-facing features you request, such as identifying the selected channel, preparing or submitting an upload, and showing its status. We will not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles. We will not use Google user data for generalized AI/ML model training. Human access to Google user data will be limited to what is necessary for support you request, security investigations, legal compliance, or other circumstances permitted by Google's policies.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can read Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

4. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide workspace functionality; process videos and generate requested results; prepare content drafts; perform an upload only when the feature is enabled and you explicitly request it; display processing or upload status; respond to support and deletion requests; prevent abuse; and comply with legal obligations.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate the service or complete an action you request. These may include Supabase (authentication and database services), Vercel (hosting and application delivery), and the video-processing provider configured for a requested job. If YouTube API features are later enabled, Google/YouTube will receive the information necessary to perform the action you authorize. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation.

We do not authorize service providers to use Google API data for advertising or generalized AI model training. We do not make Google API data available to third parties except as necessary to provide the requested feature or as permitted by Google's policies.

6. Storage, security, and retention

We use access controls and service-provider security features intended to protect account and workspace information. Credentials and API secrets are intended to remain server-side and must not be shared in public messages. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain account and workspace information while needed to provide the service and for legitimate security, operational, or legal purposes. Video-processing providers may temporarily process source media and generated outputs to complete a job; their retention is subject to their own terms. If YouTube integration is enabled, OAuth tokens and YouTube data will be retained only as needed for the authorized feature, account connection, and security, subject to applicable legal requirements and Google's policies. We will update this policy if actual retention periods or providers materially change.

7. Disconnecting Google and revoking access

When Google/YouTube account connection is made available, you may disconnect it in the application if that control is provided, or revoke Sorich Studio's access from your Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents future API access, but does not automatically remove information already stored in the workspace or content already uploaded to YouTube. Submit a separate deletion request if you also want us to delete stored workspace information.

8. Data deletion and privacy requests

You may request deletion of your Sorich Studio account-related information and workspace records by emailing alisusa169@gmail.com or using our Data Deletion Instructions page. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect your account. We will explain if any information must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping reasons.

Deleting information from Sorich Studio does not delete videos or other content already uploaded to YouTube or remove data held independently by Google or other providers. You must manage or delete that content through the relevant provider.

9. Children's privacy

The service is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone who is not legally permitted to create an account or authorize the connected services in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards or other lawful mechanisms for such transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before the changed processing is introduced where reasonably practicable.
