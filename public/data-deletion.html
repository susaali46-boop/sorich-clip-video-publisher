SORICH STUDIO

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 31, 2026

You can request deletion of personal information and workspace data associated with your Sorich Studio account.

Current status

The production app supports account and private workspace data. If you connected Google/YouTube in a configured test environment, you can revoke that access separately through Google Account permissions.

1. Request deletion from Sorich Studio

Email alisusa169@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject "Sorich Studio Data Deletion Request" and state that you want your account and associated workspace data deleted. Do not include your password or access tokens. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request.

We will review the request and delete or de-identify information we control, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and record-keeping requirements. We will explain if any information must be retained. Deletion from backups or provider systems may take additional time under their retention processes.

2. Revoke Google or YouTube access

If you authorized Google/YouTube, visit Google Account third-party connections, select Sorich Studio, and remove its access. Revocation stops future access through that authorization; it does not delete information already stored by Sorich Studio.

3. Delete content held by YouTube or Google

Deleting Sorich Studio data does not delete videos or other content already stored by YouTube or Google. Manage that content directly in your Google or YouTube account.

4. Questions

For help, contact alisusa169@gmail.com.
