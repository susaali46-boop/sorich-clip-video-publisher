Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 31, 2026

You can request deletion of personal information and workspace data associated with your Sorich Studio account. These instructions also explain how to revoke Google/YouTube access if that integration is enabled in a future release.

Current status

The current application does not yet provide a working Google/YouTube OAuth connection. If you have only created a Sorich Studio account, you can still request deletion of your account and workspace information using the contact method below.

1. Request deletion from Sorich Studio

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your Sorich Studio account to alisusa169@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line "Sorich Studio Data Deletion Request".
  3. State the account email address and that you are requesting deletion of your Sorich Studio account and associated workspace data. Do not include your password or any access token.
  4. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request.

We will review the request and delete or de-identify information that we control, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and record-keeping requirements. We will explain if any information must be retained. Deletion from backups or provider systems may take additional time under their retention processes.

2. Revoke Google or YouTube access

If you have authorized a Google/YouTube connection in a future version, you can revoke it through Google Account third-party connections. Select Sorich Studio and remove its access. Revocation stops future access through that authorization; it does not itself delete data already stored by Sorich Studio.

3. Delete content held by YouTube or Google

Deleting Sorich Studio data does not delete videos, comments, or other content already uploaded to YouTube, nor does it delete information held independently by Google. Manage or delete that content directly in your Google or YouTube account.

4. Questions

For help with a deletion request, contact alisusa169@gmail.com.
