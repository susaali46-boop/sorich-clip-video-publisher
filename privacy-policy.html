Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sorich AI ("we," "us," or "the project") is intended to handle information when you visit this website or use its planned Google Drive and TikTok publishing features. The public website is currently informational; account connection, video processing, scheduling, and publishing features are still being configured and may not yet be available.

Current status

This website does not currently provide a public registration form or an active video-upload dashboard. If and when integrations are enabled, the workflow will use permissions you grant to perform actions you request.

1. Information that may be processed

When integrations are enabled and you choose to use them, the workflow may process:

  • Basic TikTok profile information returned by Login Kit, such as a display name, avatar, and account identifier.
  • Google Drive file information needed to locate a video you select, such as its name, file identifier, type, and size.
  • The video you select and related post details you provide, including caption, hashtags, schedule, and visibility choice.
  • Upload and publishing identifiers, status, errors, and timestamps needed to report whether an action completed.
  • Technical diagnostics needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

2. How information is used

Information is intended to be used to connect the account you choose, locate and transfer a selected video, prepare post details, submit an authorized upload or post, show status, and troubleshoot failures. We do not intend to sell personal information or use connected-account data to build advertising profiles.

3. Google Drive and TikTok

Google Drive and TikTok are separate services. If you authorize access, the workflow may access data permitted by the scopes you approve. A selected video and its post details may be transmitted to TikTok when you request an upload or publication. TikTok's processing and retention are governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policies. You can review or revoke Google access at Google Account third-party connections; TikTok access can be managed through TikTok account settings.

4. Service providers

The planned workflow may rely on authorized infrastructure and integration providers, including Composio, Google, GitHub Pages, and TikTok. Those providers may process information as needed to deliver their services under their own terms. This static GitHub Pages website does not itself receive Google Drive videos or TikTok access tokens.

5. Storage and retention

Retention depends on the features ultimately enabled and the providers involved. Temporary video copies, job records, and publishing status information should be retained only as long as needed to complete the requested workflow, troubleshoot it, or meet applicable requirements. Deleting a local or workflow copy does not delete a video already published to TikTok.

6. Security

We aim to use provider authorization flows and limit access to what is needed. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or API secrets through this website, public GitHub issues, or ordinary messages.

7. Your choices and requests

You choose whether to connect accounts and which videos to submit. You can revoke provider permissions through the relevant account settings. If the service stores workflow records, you may request deletion through the project contact route below; some records may remain where required for security, legal, or provider reasons.

8. Children's privacy

Sorich AI is not designed for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the application is implemented. We will revise the date above when material changes are made. The policy must be kept aligned with the features and providers actually in use.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the project owner through the Sorich AI GitHub repository. Do not include passwords, tokens, or secrets in a public issue.
