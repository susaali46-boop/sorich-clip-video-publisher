Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy describes the personal-use video-publishing workflow operated by the owner of Sorich-clip Video Publisher (“the workflow”). The workflow is not a public service and does not offer public accounts or a public upload form.

Information processed

When the owner chooses to publish a video, the workflow may process the selected video file and related metadata, including title, description, tags, thumbnail, and visibility setting. Authorized services may also process account identifiers, authorization information, and technical data needed to complete the requested action. This website itself does not ask visitors to submit videos or create accounts.

YouTube API Services

The workflow uses YouTube API Services to support publishing and management of videos on the owner's YouTube channel. Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Google's privacy information is available in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can review and revoke the workflow's Google account access from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Revoking access may stop publishing features from working.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to carry out the owner's requested publishing workflow, maintain its operation, and troubleshoot errors. To perform an upload, the selected video and metadata are transmitted to YouTube. Depending on the configured automation, files or workflow data may also pass through connected storage or automation providers chosen by the owner. Those providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies. This site does not sell personal information or offer data to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Video files and workflow records may remain in the connected services according to the owner's settings and those providers' retention controls. Published videos and metadata remain on YouTube until the channel owner changes or deletes them. To request deletion of workflow-controlled copies or ask a question about data handling, contact the project owner through the GitHub profile and identify the data or workflow record involved. The owner will review the request and delete data they control where feasible, subject to legal, security, and provider limitations. To delete a YouTube video, use YouTube Studio; deleting a local or workflow copy does not automatically delete a YouTube upload.

Security

Access should be limited to the owner and authorized connected services. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or API secrets through the website or a public issue.

Children

This personal-use workflow is not designed for children or intended to collect information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the workflow or its connected providers change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. The owner should keep this policy accurate to the workflow actually used.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the project owner through GitHub.

Google data reminder

Google user data obtained through YouTube API Services is used only as described here and as needed for the requested functionality, and is handled in accordance with applicable YouTube API policies. Revoke Google access at Google Account permissions.
