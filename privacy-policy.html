Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy describes a personal workflow used to publish videos to the Sorich-clip YouTube channel. It is not a public service for third-party users.

Information processed

The workflow may process video files selected for upload and related publishing details, including titles, descriptions, tags, and visibility settings. If the owner selects files from connected cloud storage, the workflow may access those files and information needed to retrieve them.

Use of information

Information is used to prepare and publish videos to the channel owner's YouTube channel and operate the connected automation. This website does not have a visitor account system or upload form.

Third-party services

The workflow may use Google Drive, YouTube/Google, Make, Composio, and other services explicitly connected by the owner. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing

The workflow is not intended to sell personal information or give third parties access to upload videos. Files and metadata may be sent to connected services required for publishing. Videos may be public on YouTube when the owner selects public visibility.

Retention and security

Files and metadata may remain in connected accounts according to account settings and provider retention practices. The owner should review connected permissions periodically. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Choices

The owner can manage connected-service permissions, remove stored files, and change video visibility or delete videos using the relevant provider controls.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the workflow or its data practices change. For questions about this personal project, visit the project owner's GitHub profile.

Important: This policy must be updated if actual data practices differ from this description. Publishing it does not guarantee YouTube API quota approval.
